# TrustScan: Brand and Naming

## Recommended name: TrustScan

**Tagline:** Verify true second opinions.

Why it works:
- Short, memorable, and easy to say in a meeting or pitch
- "Trust" names the core question: can we trust AI agreement?
- Fits healthcare and governance settings

Things to check before using it:
- **Trademark and existing products.** Search for other companies or products called TrustScan before committing.
- **"Scan" can suggest the product reads scans or diagnoses.** TrustScan only monitors AI tools, so the copy should always say it flags cases for human review.

## Descriptive name

**AI independence monitor** is the preferred descriptive subtitle, for example "TrustScan: AI independence monitor." It explains the idea without suggesting that TrustScan itself creates model diversity.

## Alternatives considered

| Name | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| TrueSecond | Directly references the "second opinion" idea | Sounds more like a consumer service |
| SafeConsensus | Says that agreement is not always safe | Longer, and "consensus" is abstract |
| CorrelateCare | Combines correlation and care | Technical, harder to say |
| Checkpoint Clinical | Audit-friendly, enterprise feel | Generic |
| ModelCheck Health | Clear AI validation framing | Generic and forgettable |

## Voice

Plain, calm, and specific. Avoid fear language and absolute claims such as "no one is auditing" or "AI agreement is not trustworthy." Prefer "may," "suggest," and "consider" in clinical wording, because TrustScan advises and clinicians decide.
